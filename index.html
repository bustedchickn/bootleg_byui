New Student Orientation

New Student Orientation

Come see what BYU-Idaho is all about. New Student Orientation introduces you to the culture and climate of BYU-Idaho with lots of fun events.

Get ready for your college experience by learning what the campus is like and all the available resources to help you succeed.

Fall 2025 On-Campus Orientation Schedule
Thursday, September 11th to Saturday, September 13th
You may park on campus during New Student Orientation without a permit. However, parking permits are required the day classes start.
